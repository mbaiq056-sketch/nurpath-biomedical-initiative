01 / About us

Science should create
possibilities for everyone.

We believe that science should serve people. Biomedical progress has the greatest value when it responds to real health needs and creates possibilities for communities everywhere.

NURPATH Biomedical Initiative is an emerging, mission-driven initiative exploring how responsible biomedical research and health innovation can contribute to a more accessible and equitable future.

Our vision brings together scientific inquiry, interdisciplinary collaboration, and a commitment to keeping people at the centre of innovation. We recognise that meaningful progress requires rigorous evidence, responsible development, and an understanding of the communities research aims to serve.

As NURPATH develops, we seek to connect with researchers, institutions, and communities who share the belief that better health should be a collective ambition.

02 / Research

From scientific questions
to meaningful possibilities.

Exploratory research direction

Exploring biosensing approaches for earlier cancer detection

Early detection is an important challenge in cancer care. NURPATH is interested in exploring how biomedical research and biosensing technologies might contribute to future approaches for earlier detection.

This is an early-stage concept for future exploration. No clinically validated biosensor or diagnostic product is currently claimed by the initiative.

Our approach: Research directions are exploratory and will require scientific evidence, appropriate validation, and responsible development before any clinical application can be considered.

A

Biomedical discovery

03 / Founder

FOUNDER & INITIATOR

Baiq Mira
Nurfatihah

“Science should open doors to better health—not create greater distance from it.”

NURPATH Biomedical Initiative began with a simple belief: scientific progress matters most when it contributes to the well-being of people and communities.

Founded by Baiq Mira Nurfatihah, NURPATH is an emerging initiative committed to connecting biomedical research, responsible innovation, and the pursuit of more accessible health technologies.

The initiative seeks to encourage collaboration across scientific disciplines and explore how research can respond to real health needs, particularly in underserved communities.

06 / Partnerships

Better questions.
Stronger together.

NURPATH believes that meaningful progress in biomedical research grows through shared knowledge, thoughtful collaboration, and a commitment to improving human well-being.

As an emerging initiative, we welcome conversations with researchers, universities, healthcare professionals, community organisations, and others who share our interest in responsible science and more accessible health innovation.

AREAS FOR POTENTIAL COLLABORATION

07 / Our principles

Ambitious in discovery.
Grounded in responsibility.

The way research is conducted should reflect the impact we hope it can make.

01

Scientific integrity

Transparent methods, reproducibility, evidence-based conclusions, and honest communication of limitations.

02

Human-centred innovation

Consider people, contexts, safety, usability, and practical needs throughout research and development.

03

Equitable access

Work toward a future where biomedical progress can benefit communities beyond those with the greatest resources.

04

Responsible collaboration

Build partnerships around shared expectations, mutual respect, and appropriate research governance.
