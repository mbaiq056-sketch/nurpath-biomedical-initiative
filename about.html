ABOUT US

The people and purpose behind NURPATH.

We believe meaningful biomedical progress begins with people, grows through collaboration, and aims to improve lives.

OUR FOUNDER

Science with purpose. Health with people at its centre.

Baiq Mira Nurfatihah

Founder & Initiator

NURPATH Biomedical Initiative began with a belief that scientific progress should contribute to human well-being and create possibilities for more accessible health innovation.

Founded by Baiq Mira Nurfatihah, NURPATH is an emerging initiative exploring connections between biomedical research, responsible innovation, and the health needs of communities.

The initiative aims to encourage interdisciplinary dialogue and future collaboration around research that can serve a meaningful public purpose.

OUR TEAM

Better progress, built together.

NURPATH believes that meaningful progress comes from shared knowledge, diverse perspectives, and responsible collaboration.

As the initiative develops, we welcome conversations with researchers, students, healthcare professionals, universities, and community organisations who share our interest in biomedical research and more accessible health innovation.

NURPATH is currently developing its collaborative network. This page will evolve as contributors formally join the initiative.
