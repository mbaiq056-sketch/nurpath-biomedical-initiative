01 / Our founder

THE PERSON BEHIND THE INITIATIVE

Baiq Mira Nurfatihah

Science with purpose. Health with people at its centre.

NURPATH Biomedical Initiative began with a belief that scientific progress should contribute to human well-being and create possibilities for more accessible health innovation.

Founded by Baiq Mira Nurfatihah, NURPATH is an emerging initiative exploring connections between biomedical research, responsible innovation, and the health needs of communities.

Its long-term direction is to encourage interdisciplinary dialogue and explore research questions that may contribute to meaningful public benefit.

03 / What guides us

A clearer purpose.
A more human future.

Our direction is guided by the belief that scientific progress should be rigorous, responsible, and centred on human well-being.

01

Scientific integrity

We value transparent methods, evidence-based conclusions, reproducibility, and honest communication of limitations.

02

Human-centred innovation

We believe safety, usability, affordability, and community needs should matter when considering future health technologies.

03

Responsible collaboration

We welcome knowledge exchange and future collaboration grounded in mutual respect, appropriate governance, and shared purpose.
